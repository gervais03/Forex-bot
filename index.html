Gold Signal Desk

XAU/USD signals from 5-minute candles, checked against the 1-hour trend. You place and close the trades in MT5.

Setup

Spread is what MT5 charges you on each gold trade. Look at the spread in MT5 and type it here. It is counted in the simulated results, and no signal is given if it is more than a quarter of the stop. ADX measures trend strength. Run the backtest below before turning it on.

On a big news day (Fed, jobs report) enter the release time. Signals stop for 30 minutes before and after it.

Type your MT5 price and tap Match. The page works out the gap to its own price feed and shifts every level into MT5 prices. Do this just before you trade, and again if the gap may have changed.

Every signal is tracked from its signal price with its own stop and target, whether you recorded it or not. A candle that touches both levels counts as a loss.

Back up: tap Back up, then Copy, and paste the text into your Notes app. Restore: paste it here again and tap Restore. Your API key is not included. Safari can erase a page's saved data after about a week without use, so add this page to your Home Screen and keep a backup.

Signals come from indicators on past prices. They can lose. Test on a demo account first.
